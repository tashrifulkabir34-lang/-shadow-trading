1. Paper Overview & Contribution

Mehta, Reeb & Zhao (2020) document shadow trading: corporate insiders exploit private information by trading in economically-linked firms (suppliers, customers, competitors) rather than their own — circumventing SEC insider trading restrictions. The paper finds three key results:

Result 1 — Existence
Informed trading in linked firms spikes in the 30-day window before source firm earnings/M&A/product announcements. 1 SD increase in BP CAR → 6.4%–19.2% more informed trading.
Result 2 — Mechanism
Shadow trading increases after high-profile SEC enforcement actions (substitution effect) and after IDD legal shocks (employee mobility channel). Rules out sophisticated-investor / market-friction alternatives.
Result 3 — Policing
Corporate prohibition policies attenuate shadow trading by ~8–12% (business partners). Only ~53% of firms explicitly prohibit it. No clear U.S. legal precedent for prosecution.
